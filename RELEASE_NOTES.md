# Release notes

All notable changes to this project are listed here, one entry per commit,
newest first.

## 1.0.0 — First stable release

s-INK for Trello brings your Trello boards to Supernote devices (optimised
for the Manta) and links them to your handwritten notes.

- **Copy-free Trello sign-in**: scan a QR code or open the browser, press
  Allow, and the token comes back end-to-end encrypted.
- **Token lifetime and storage**: 1 day, 30 days or no limit, kept in
  memory or saved on the tablet; Disconnect revokes it.
- **Boards page**: Trello-style tiles with backgrounds, starred boards
  first, and public boards such as Supernote's roadmap.
- **Boards made for E Ink**: pages turned instead of scrolled, up to 3
  lists side by side in portrait and 4 in landscape.
- **Focus and Review modes**: show only the cards that matter, by labels,
  members and completion.
- **Card menu**: a long press on a card offers Open, Start work, Link in
  note, Mark complete and Archive.
- **Card page**: edit labels, members, completion, checklists and
  description, write comments and attach files from the tablet.
- **New Trello Card from the lasso**: handwriting becomes a card, and
  hand-drawn squares become checklist items.
- **s-INK links in notes**: link handwriting to a card or board; a tap on
  the link opens it.
- **Start work**: assign yourself, change labels, move the card and create
  its note, in one confirmed step.
- **Card notes**: one folder per board, each note tied to its card even
  when the card is renamed.
- **Migrations**: after an update, s-INK offers to bring older data up to
  date.
